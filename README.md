# LLM Evaluation Portfolio

### Evidence-Based AI Quality Assessment

This portfolio documents my hands-on practice evaluating AI-generated responses, agent workflows, and visual outputs.

I am transitioning into Artificial Intelligence after completing the **IBM Generative AI Engineering Professional Certificate**, with a focus on LLM Evaluation, AI Quality Assurance, and AI Training.

My previous experience in photography, entrepreneurship, and client-facing services brings an additional perspective to quality assessment, visual consistency, problem-solving, and attention to detail.

## Evaluation Areas

**1. LLM Response Evaluation**

Assessing instruction following, factuality, relevance, groundedness, reasoning, and clarity.

**2. AI Agent & Tool Evaluation**

Reviewing agent actions, tool results, unsupported claims, and discrepancies between executed actions and final responses.

**3. Visual AI Evaluation**

Assessing prompt adherence, image consistency, visual defects, lighting, composition, and realism.

## My AI-First Approach

I use AI tools to support learning, development, and documentation while maintaining human oversight of evaluation decisions.

My approach emphasizes evidence-based reasoning, transparent criteria, verification, and responsible AI use.
