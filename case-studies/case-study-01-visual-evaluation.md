# Case Study 01: Visual AI Evaluation

**Evaluation Type:** Prompt Alignment and Visual Quality Assessment  
**Environment:** Simulated training exercise  
**Final Decision:** REJECT

## Objective

Evaluate whether an AI-generated product photograph meets the user's requirements and identify visual inconsistencies.

## Original User Prompt

> Create a professional product photograph of a red ceramic coffee mug on a wooden table. The mug must have a white interior, a visible handle on the right side, and no text or logos. Use soft natural window lighting with realistic shadows.

## Evaluation Evidence

The supplied inspection report described:

- A red ceramic mug with a white interior on a wooden table.
- A handle positioned on the left instead of the right.
- A small black logo near the bottom of the mug.
- Two shadows extending in opposite directions.

## Evaluation Findings

| Criterion | Finding | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Color and material | Red ceramic | Pass |
| Interior | White | Pass |
| Handle position | Left instead of right | Fail |
| Branding | Logo present | Fail |
| Shadows | Inconsistent directions | Fail |

## Evaluator's Original Assessment

**Prompt Alignment Errors:**

- Red ceramic
- A visible handle on the right side
- No logos
- Realistic shadows

**Visual Quality Issues:**

- Glossy red ceramic
- The handle is clearly visible on the left side
- Small black logo
- Two shadows in opposite directions, suggesting inconsistent light sources

**Final Decision:** Reject

## Conclusion

The output fails to meet explicit user requirements regarding handle placement and branding. The inconsistent shadows also undermine the requested visual realism.

The glossy ceramic finish is not itself a violation because the prompt does not specify a surface finish.

**Decision: REJECT**

## Methodology Note

This exercise was conducted using a written visual inspection report rather than direct examination of an image. The findings therefore reflect the evidence provided in that report.
